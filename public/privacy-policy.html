Safe Card Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Safe Card is a multiplayer card game developed by Eric Kimani.

1. Information We Collect

Safe Card may process information needed to provide the game's features, including:

  • Player names (optional - you can play anonymously)
  • Game and room information
  • Technical information required for the app to function
  • Device identifiers (for game sessions)

Important: Safe Card does not require users to create an account or provide their real name. You can enjoy the full game experience without sharing personal information.

2. Advertising

Safe Card uses Google AdMob to display advertisements in the game.

AdMob may collect and use information such as:

  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • General location information

This information is used to provide, measure, and improve advertising services. Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws (such as GDPR or CCPA), you may be asked for consent before personalized advertising is provided.

Learn more about Google's advertising practices: Google Ads Privacy Policy

3. Online Multiplayer Features

When you use online multiplayer features in Safe Card, certain information is transmitted to our backend services to create and operate game rooms. This may include:

  • Room code
  • Player name (if provided)
  • Game state and moves
  • Connection information

This data is processed only to provide the multiplayer experience and is not shared with third parties except as described in this policy.

4. Children's Privacy

Safe Card is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children, and we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information used by Safe Card. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We are committed to protecting your privacy, but we cannot guarantee absolute security of transmitted data over the internet.

6. Third-Party Services

Safe Card uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: For online game functionality, database management, and real-time features
  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy & Security

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to request deletion of your data
  • Right to opt-out of personalized advertising
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address below.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date. Your continued use of Safe Card after any modifications constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
